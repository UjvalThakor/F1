Born into a racing dynasty, Max began karting at just four years old under the mentorship of his father, ex-F1 driver Jos Verstappen. By 2013, he achieved a historic double, winning both the European and World KZ Championships at age 15—one of the most dominant karting records in motorsport history.

In 2015, Max debuted with Scuderia Toro Rosso at just 17 years and 166 days as the youngest driver in F1 history. Promoted to Red Bull Racing ahead of the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix, he delivered an astonishing drive to claim victory on his team debut, becoming the youngest Grand Prix winner at 18 years and 228 days.

In 2021, Verstappen engaged in one of the most intense title battles in Formula 1 history. Driving the Red Bull RB16B, he claimed 10 wins, 18 podiums, and 10 pole positions. With a thrilling last-lap overtake at Abu Dhabi, he clinched his maiden FIA Formula 1 World Championship, becoming the first Dutch champion.

In Formula 1’s ground-effect era, Verstappen achieved unparalleled dominance. In 2023, he shattered records with 19 wins in 22 races and 10 consecutive victories. In 2024, he clinched his fourth consecutive World Drivers' Championship, joining Fangio, Prost, Schumacher, Vettel, and Hamilton among racing legends.

With over 60 Grand Prix victories, four consecutive World Drivers' Championships, and surgical precision in all conditions, Max Verstappen continues to define the pinnacle of Formula 1. Relentless, uncompromising, and faster than ever, his legacy is already etched in motorsport history.
